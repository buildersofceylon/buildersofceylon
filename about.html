§ 04 · The company

A handful of people.

We're a small team — about a dozen technicians across six trades, one workshop, one number. That's the company.

Builders of Ceylon started because two of us spent a weekend trying to find a plumber and an electrician who would (a) answer, (b) turn up, and (c) not invent a fault that wasn't there.

We failed at all three. So we built the company we'd wanted to call that weekend.

Today we cover the entire Western Province — Colombo, Gampaha, Kalutara — with a team of trained technicians in plumbing, electrical, painting, masonry, equipment repair, and air conditioning. One number, one promise, one bill.

We don't take orders on the website yet. Everything goes through WhatsApp, because that's where Sri Lanka actually lives. Tap your problem, hit send, we take it from there.
