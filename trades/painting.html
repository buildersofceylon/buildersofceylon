The work

How we approach painting.

Most paint jobs fail because of the wall, not the paint. We prep properly: scrape, sand, prime, fill — then put on the colour you actually picked.

We use Dulux, Multilac and Nippon — your choice. Two coats, even coverage, sharp edges around switches and frames.

Furniture is covered, floors are protected, and we clean up before we leave. You walk back into a finished room, not a building site.
