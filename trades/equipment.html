The work

How we approach equipment repair.

The replace-everything culture is wasteful. Most appliances die from one €5 part — a worn bearing, a tired capacitor, a fried thermostat. We replace the part, not the machine.

Bring it to our workshop or we'll come to you, whichever is cheaper. You'll get a fixed quote before any disassembly so you can decide if it's worth it.

Six-month workmanship guarantee on every repair. If it breaks the same way, we fix it free.
