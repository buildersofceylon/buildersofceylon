The work

How we approach electrical.

Electrical work is the trade where corners cost lives. Every job we do follows the rules — earthed properly, sized properly, terminated cleanly.

If a socket is sparking, a breaker keeps tripping, or there's a smell of burning anywhere in your house, that's a same-day visit. Tell us and we'll be there.

For bigger work — new wiring, switchboard upgrades, inverter installs — you get a fixed quote with a written scope before anything is touched.
