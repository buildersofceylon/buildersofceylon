§ 04 · Common questions

Questions, briefly.

How fast do you respond?

WhatsApp messages are answered within minutes during working hours (). For non-emergencies we'll usually have a technician at your door inside an hour.

How much does it cost?

Diagnosis is free if you proceed with the repair. The technician will give you a fixed price before any work starts — no surprises. Pay only after the job is done.

What if the repair doesn't work?

You get a full refund. We'd rather lose the job than your trust.

Do you bring parts?

For most small repairs, yes. For replacements (taps, switches, fittings) we'll quote you the part separately and you choose the brand.

Which areas do you cover?

Colombo, Gampaha and Kalutara — the entire Western Province. See coverage for details.

Can I just call instead of WhatsApp?

Yes — . But WhatsApp is faster because you can send a photo.
