§ 03 · The promise

Five things we owe you.

These are the rules we hold ourselves to — every job, every customer, every district.

Refund if not fixed.

If you're not happy with the repair, you don't pay. We'd rather lose the job than your trust.

Response within the hour.

Message us during working hours and a uniformed technician will usually be at your door inside 60 minutes.

Written quote before work.

You see the price before anything is touched. No "we'll let you know at the end" — that's how customers get burned.

Uniformed technicians.

Forest-green polo, ID card, clean tools. You'll know it's us at the gate. No one shows up unannounced or unbranded.

Cleaned up before we leave.

Tools packed, debris bagged, surfaces wiped. You walk back into a finished room, not a building site.

Six-month workmanship guarantee.

If the same thing breaks the same way within six months, we come back and fix it free. No questions.

Why we exist

Because the wait is the worst part.

Anyone who's ever tried to find a plumber in Colombo on a Sunday knows the routine. Three calls, two "I'll come tomorrow"s, one no-show, and the bathroom is still flooded.

Builders of Ceylon exists because home repair shouldn't feel like a favour. It should feel like a service — predictable, professional, priced fairly, with someone polite at the gate when they said they'd be there.

That's all we're really selling: the relief of knowing someone is actually coming.

Hold us to it.

If we ever fall short of one of these, tell us — and we'll make it right.
